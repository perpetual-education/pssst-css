# PSSST

House conventions: `../CLAUDE.md`. What pssst is for: README.md.

## What goes in

Pssst teaches what we do and why: a way in for people to understand it, try it, adopt it, or teach us back. It's also the starting template for new projects. We build real sites with it, compare what they each work out, and what holds up comes back here.

The bar for anything coming back:

- It reads in order: the import order is the lesson.
- Every rule carries its reason, in a comment, in plain words.
- Each thing has an example of why it's there: what goes wrong without it.

True but not yet explainable that simply? It stays in the site that found it until it is.
